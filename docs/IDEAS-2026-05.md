## Demo-wow picks (AI, all achievable on the wired Bedrock integration)

1. **Order Concierge** — "build my usual order, swap anything banned or out of stock": Claude tool-use loop over the existing hybrid search, order history, and restriction checks → reviewable draft cart with pgvector-ranked compliant substitutes. The most theatrical buyer-side demo; every piece is already wired.
2. **Forward-an-email price-sheet ingestion** — photograph a messy distributor PDF on stage, watch the extraction pipeline emit a reviewable pricing diff with confidence scores. Lands hardest with distributor prospects.
3. **Rep Cockpit AI order capture** — paste a voicemail transcript ("3 cs blue razz 5k, 2 boxes king cones, same juice as last time") → resolved, tier-priced order lines with ambiguous items flagged. Visceral for the persona spending 4–6 h/day rekeying orders.

## Gas-station owner — persona-specific shortlist

The pains, in their words, and what we build:

1. **True Landed Cost** — tobacco ≈35% of inside sales; BEST PRICE on raw wholesale silently loses 3–5 margin points in percent-of-wholesale states.
2. **POS sales import + price-book export loop** (Verifone Commander / Gilbarco Passport / Clover) — sales data sits unread in the back office; owners re-key cost/retail changes UPC-by-UPC for 30–60 min after every delivery. This pair is the data spine for everything else.
3. **Margin Workbench + silent margin-compression alerts** — a 6% mid-quarter cost increase with an unchanged shelf tag erases $400+/month; reuses `AnomalyRecord` + `PriceHistory`.
4. **Reorder Optimizer** — case-pack rounding, tier-break nudges ("order 5, not 3, cross the 24+ break, save $0.40/unit"), open-to-buy cash cap.
5. **Morning Store Pulse** — one-screen daily brief + one-tap fill-cart, replacing the legal pad, the cooler walk, and three phone calls.
6. **Cash-Out Timeline at checkout** — the defining squeeze is a 3-day fuel EFT vs Net30/prepay inventory; show when each dollar leaves, flag stacked weeks, suggest swapping a line to a Net30 supplier.
7. **Dead-Stock Radar** — typically $8–15k frozen in non-movers; flag, price the markdown ladder, suppress re-suggestion.
8. **Clerk Mode receiving** — receiving is done by whoever is on register, interrupted mid-truck; PIN sub-logins + pause/resume so receipts stop rotting in PARTIAL_RECEIVED past claim windows.
9. **Scan-data/buydown rebate reconciliation** — $3,000–8,000/yr of Altria/RJR/ITG rebates left unclaimed. Highest-dollar unsolved pain; XL effort — flagged for later, do not start before the spine (idea 2) exists.
